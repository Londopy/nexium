Q: `nx run`: what does it print?
| fn main() {
|     let small: u8 = 250
|     println("{} {}", .{small +% 10, small +| 10})
| }
ask: output
- 260 260
* 4 255
- 4 4
> `+%` wraps around 256 and `+|` stops at the largest `u8`; neither can panic, so `main` has no `panics` from them.
