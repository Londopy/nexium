Q: After `let b = a` where `a` is a `List(i32)`, what is `a`?
* moved: using it is a compile error
- a copy of `b`
- an alias of `b`
> Owning values move on assignment; `.clone()` is the explicit copy.

Q: A function takes `xs: List(String)` without `own`. What may it do with `xs`?
* read it
- move it into a struct
- drop it early
> Parameters are borrowed: the callee reads, the caller keeps the value. `own` hands it over.

Q: Which view does the compiler reject since 1.0?
* returning a slice of a local from the function that owns it
- returning a slice of a parameter
- storing a slice of a literal in a global
> Rule R1 (now V1): the local dies with the function. A parameter's storage belongs to the caller, and a literal lives as long as the program.
